# PT650 Platform 2035 — Product & Economy Charter

## Product identity

PT650 is not only a workout logger. It is a daily sports network connecting athletes, coaches,
gyms, equipment manufacturers, recovery providers, retailers, sponsors and events.

**Core promise:** train, move or contribute real verified value and receive useful value back.

## Earned Access

The athlete does not need a paid subscription while providing useful, verified participation.
Advanced services can be unlocked through meaningful activity or contribution. A user who prefers
not to participate may buy access directly.

Safety-critical exercise guidance and the user's own data are not held behind a contribution wall.

## Verified Activity Economy

Rewards are earned for real outcomes such as verified walking/running goals, equipment use,
high-quality equipment contributions, sponsor research tasks, event participation or other
explicitly defined achievements.

Every mission must show before the user joins:

- the exact goal;
- start/end time;
- verification method;
- exact reward or benefit;
- sponsor;
- any eligibility/quantity limit;
- what data is used.

When a finite sponsored reward is offered, PT650 reserves funding at enrollment. A user must never
finish an advertised goal and then discover that the advertised reward disappeared.

## PT650 Move

Walking and running are first-class PT650 activities so the platform includes people who do not
belong to gyms.

Examples include sponsored step goals, verified GPS routes, corporate wellness challenges,
tourism/walking routes and event preparation. The product language is outcome-based:

> Achieve the stated goal. Once verification succeeds, receive the stated reward.

No lottery wording, hidden conversion rate or moving redemption threshold.

## Equipment Network

Machine Scan, manufacturer QR/NFC, PT650 QR and manual verification resolve into one Equipment
Passport:

`manufacturer -> model -> physical machine -> venue -> verified use -> athlete history`

This supports technique guidance, personal machine settings, maintenance reports, equipment
discovery, gym inventory and manufacturer intelligence.

Manufacturer value may include aggregated distribution maps, verified usage, repeat usage,
quality feedback, maintenance patterns and product research. Individual athlete location/history
is not exposed to manufacturers.

## Manufacturer missions

Manufacturers may sponsor verified trials and structured research. Reward eligibility is based on
completion and evidence quality. Positive and negative reviews receive the same treatment.

A sponsor may buy research access or campaign distribution. A sponsor may not buy a rating.

## Coach economy

Qualified coaches can sell remote programs, asynchronous form reviews, live video sessions,
group sessions and, as interfaces mature, AR/VR/Mixed-Reality sessions.

Coach incentives reward service quality signals such as verified qualifications, attendance,
retention, completed programs and legitimate client outcomes. PT650 must never reward a coach for
producing more claims, recommending more products, or giving a sponsor a favorable opinion.

AI handles routine analysis and preparation; humans remain available for judgment, coaching and
relationship-intensive work.

## Marketplace surfaces

Potential monetisation surfaces include:

- sports nutrition, equipment, wearables and accessories marketplace/affiliate commerce;
- meal-prep and grocery fulfilment from Nutrition AI plans;
- gym day/week/month passes and instant booking;
- traveller-focused Train Anywhere matching against today's workout equipment needs;
- sports massage, physiotherapy, recovery centres, sauna/spa and appropriate sports-clinic booking;
- coaches, live sessions and future VR/AR sessions;
- races, walking events, camps and registrations;
- corporate wellness programs;
- sponsored verified-activity campaigns;
- manufacturer analytics, research missions, APIs and B2B leads;
- gym SaaS for equipment QR, utilisation and maintenance workflows;
- equipment maintenance and spare-parts marketplace;
- verified used commercial gym equipment;
- B2B equipment procurement intelligence;
- privacy-preserving aggregate research products.

PT650 should prefer commissions, B2B SaaS and sponsored verified activity over making the athlete
the primary source of revenue.

## PT650 Trust Protocol

1. No reward for a positive opinion.
2. No sponsor can buy or suppress an Equipment Score.
3. Reward terms are frozen before participation begins.
4. Objective verified achievements settle immediately when the challenge promises instant reward.
5. Suspicious activity can enter review, but the review rule must be disclosed before enrollment.
6. Raw GPS/photos use minimum necessary retention.
7. Personal health/location data is not sold to advertisers or manufacturers.
8. Sponsored placements and sponsored research are clearly labelled.
9. Contribution rewards depend on evidence quality and completeness, not volume spam.
10. Safety guidance is never withheld to force engagement or payment.

## 2035 interfaces

The data model should remain usable when the interface changes from a phone to watches, ambient
sensors, live video, AR glasses, VR training rooms or smart gym equipment.

A future smart machine may receive the athlete's approved seat/load/set prescription and return
reps, range of motion, tempo and completion. PT650 should treat those as another verified event
source rather than redesigning the economy around a particular hardware generation.


## Health and endurance reference

Endurain is an approved **functional and architectural reference** for activity ingestion, activity
streams/laps, Garmin and Strava integrations, FIT/GPX/TCX import, health steps, sleep, weight,
hydration, fasting, health targets, athlete gear and calendar concepts.

PT650 does not become an Endurain fork. The PT650 provider layer remains vendor-neutral and adds
HealthKit, Android Health Connect and Huawei adapters independently. Imported/provider observations
also remain distinct from reward-verified evidence.
